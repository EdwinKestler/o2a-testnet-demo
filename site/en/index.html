Disposable demonstration · rehearsal network

O2A — demonstration on Bitcoin signet

The first O2A EntityID is minted live. Its seal is already funded and has 6 confirmations. On stage the artist signs the genesis, the EntityID appears immediately, two verifiers report CURRENT, the artist signs the official name, and the package is restored from backup.

Signet is the rehearsal network. The network of a public event remains a maintainer decision.

It is not in production. This demonstration is not a release, and mainnet is out of scope.
It creates no persistent identity. Today's identities are tests and will be discarded.
It does not replace ticketing. It makes no claim to replace today's ticket-sales systems.

Every identity created by this demonstration is disposable. Before creation, every participant is given this warning and must explicitly acknowledge it.

01 / The sequence

What you will see

Three acts show the genesis, two CURRENT checks, and the official name. ADR-0009 keeps transitions off stage until the RGB program is final. An attestation is outside the frozen set. A claim spends no Bitcoin.

Act 1 · identity

What you see

The artist signs the genesis on stage. The EntityID and its QR code appear at that moment.

What happens underneath

The seal was funded at least 48 hours ahead and already has 6 confirmations. Signing uses rgb-protocol 0.11.1 with an Opret close. The root signs, and signer_entity is 32 zero bytes.

Act 2 · agreement

What you see

Two verifiers, on two laptops, report CURRENT. If they disagree, both lines stay on the page.

What happens underneath

Each laptop runs rgb011-check signet-verify with the seed unset. Ideally they use different Bitcoin backends. Rehearsal 3 used two directories on one machine.

Act 3 · name

What you see

The artist signs the official name. Another machine, without the seed, restores the package and shows the same EntityID.

What happens underneath

The official_name claim is a signed object. No Bitcoin transaction is broadcast for the name. The stage stops there.

Decisions still open

Every chip is still open. The text is a placeholder, not a decision. D2 is the official_name claim. D5 is the pre-funded seal. Those two are no longer in this list.

03 / The case

Why it matters

The commercial framing is still open. These are the points the script leaves ready for that conversation.

Fake promoters

The official name is signed by this identity, or it is not. That can be checked without calling anyone.

The artist's keys

The identity is keys the artist controls. Recovery is 2 of 3 after a delay. This stage does not rotate a key.

One statement

The audience leaves with the EntityID and the signed name. A second actor is still open, and the placeholder is none.

Portability

The package is checked on another machine, without asking an O2A server for permission.

05 / Try it

Verify it yourself

The event has not produced a public package. Rehearsal 3 is in evidence/signet-block0-rehearsal-3-2026-09-28.

The 0.11.1 check is spikes/rgb-0.11.1/target/debug/rgb011-check signet-verify:

env -u O2A_DEMO_SEED_FILE -u O2A_DEMO_ENTITY \
RGB_CHAIN=signet \
RGB011_EVIDENCE=PACKAGE_DIR \
spikes/rgb-0.11.1/target/debug/rgb011-check signet-verify

The command also needs BITCOIN_RPC, BITCOIN_COOKIE, and ELECTRUM. The package contains genesis.o2a, genesis.strict, and public.txt. With the name, it also contains claim.o2a.

06 / Conversation

Questions

Is this crypto or tokens?

There is no O2A token. Bitcoin anchors the EntityID seal and the order of that output.

What does each signature cost?

The official name is a signed object and costs no Bitcoin transaction. The seal is funded ahead of the show. Signet coins have no value. Mainnet is out of scope.

Why RGB?

RGB carries later transitions once the program is final. The stage claim pays no Bitcoin transaction. The Bitcoin seal is the EntityID's anchor.

What if the artist loses their key?

Recovery is 2 of 3, with a delay. The default offer is 1008 blocks. Any two of the three recovery keys can act after that delay. Recovery is not performed on stage.

Why not use our database?

The two laptops read the same package without querying an O2A service.

When will this be in production?

No date is promised. ADR-0009 keeps transitions off a frozen identity until the RGB program is final. This page is not a release.
